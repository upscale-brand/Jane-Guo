My approach

Food is more than fuel. It carries culture, comfort, joy, and connection — and nutrition care should make room for all of it.

Build habits that support your health without giving up the foods and traditions you love.

Jane combines nutrition counseling with principles of behavioral science to help people make realistic changes that last. Rather than strict diets or quick fixes, her approach considers your health goals, food preferences, culture, routines, and the realities of everyday life.

Jane’s story

Food has always been a way to show care.

Jane’s connection to food began with family, culture, and the memories built around meals. That perspective continues to shape the way she supports people from all walks of life.

Growing up

Food, family, and tradition

Growing up, Jane saw food as a love language — a way to care for others, celebrate, and create memories. Traditional Chinese food was part of that story.

A new perspective

Pride in cultural foods

Over time, Jane developed a deeper appreciation for her own heritage and the foods of cultures around the world. She believes familiar foods can have a place in a balanced way of eating.

Today

Nutrition care built around you

As founder of Habits Nutrition Counseling, Jane combines dietetics and behavior-change strategies to help clients build habits that support their individual health goals.

The Habits approach

Small changes. Habits that stay with you.

Fad diets and strict rules can be difficult to maintain. Jane focuses on gradual, manageable changes that fit your routines and preferences, helping you build a healthier way of eating over time.

  • Build routines that fit your lifestyle
